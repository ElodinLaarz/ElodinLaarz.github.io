Before you join

Does this install the game?

Join the group first. Once Google approves the beta, opt in on Google Play, then redeem your individual free code before installing. Use the same Google account for the group, Play opt-in and code. The Play link may be unavailable while review is pending.

What will you email me?

Beta invitations, test-build news and occasional Idle Microbes updates. You can leave the group or change your email preferences at any time through Google Groups.

Is the beta free?

Beta testers can request a free code for this paid game. After joining the group, email support@formalizedchaos.com from your Google account and ask for a beta code. We issue one code per person while supplies last; delivery is handled individually. Joining the group alone does not remove Google Play’s price. If Play asks you to pay, wait for your code.

What are the free-code terms?

No purchase is required. You must be at least 13 and in a country where the beta is available. Each code can be redeemed once for Idle Microbes through Google Play; it has no cash value and cannot be sold. Codes are limited to 500 in this batch and must be redeemed before April 6, 2027. Beta installation also requires group membership, Play opt-in and Google’s approval of the release. Please keep your code private.

Already using a test build?

If you joined the Play internal test, leave that test before opting into this closed beta. If you installed through Obtainium, back up your game save before changing installation channels; Play uses a different signing key.

Who handles my signup?

Google Groups manages your membership and email preferences. Formalized Chaos uses your group membership for beta invitations and game updates. Group owners and managers can see your membership and email address. Signup happens on Google Groups; this page has no email form or analytics. Google’s privacy policy explains how Google handles your information.
