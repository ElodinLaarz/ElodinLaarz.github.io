Join the mailing list ↗

On Google Groups, select Join group to finish signing up. Keep the subscription set to Each email to receive invitations in your inbox.

Joining the list is free. We’ll share the Play opt-in link when the closed beta opens.

Before you join

Does this install the game?

Mailing-list signup comes first. When the closed beta opens, use the Play link we share to become a tester and install the game. Use the same Google account for both steps.

What will you email me?

Beta invitations, test-build news and occasional Idle Microbes updates. You can leave the group or change your email preferences at any time through Google Groups.

Is the beta free?

The mailing list is free. Idle Microbes is a paid game on Google Play. We’ll explain beta access and any available promo codes before asking you to install.

Who handles my signup?

Google Groups manages your membership and email preferences. Formalized Chaos uses your group membership for beta invitations and game updates. Group owners and managers can see your membership and email address. Signup happens on Google Groups; this page has no email form or analytics. Google’s privacy policy explains how Google handles your information.
