Idle Microbes Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Idle Microbes is a game published by Formalized Chaos ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and its beta feedback form handle and what happens to it. It covers the game on Android, sold through Google Play, and on Windows and Linux, sold through Steam.

Summary

Idle Microbes has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking code. It does not use your microphone, camera, photos, or files. We never receive your game progress. The only information the game sends to us is a feedback note, and only when you choose to send one.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, your settings (such as volume and motion preferences), and a record of completed runs in the game's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. The Start Over option deletes your saved progress.

On Android, if backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account. We never receive that copy. You can turn backup off or delete backups in your device and Google account settings. Uninstalling the game deletes the data from the device.

On a computer, the data stays in the game's folder in your user profile until you delete it. Uninstalling the game through Steam may leave that folder in place. The game also writes a log of technical messages to that folder, and the log stays on your computer. If you contact us about a problem, we may ask you to send the log; sending it is up to you.

Feedback notes

The game includes an optional "leave a note" feature. If you write a note and send it, the game sends:

  • the text of your note, and
  • a short context line: the game version, the operating system name (for example "Android"), the number of cells in your colony, and which part of the game you have reached.

If a note cannot be sent right away, for example because you are offline, the game keeps it on your device and tries again later. A note sent this way also includes the date and time (in UTC) when you wrote it.

The note is not linked to your name, email address, Google or Steam account, or any device identifier. Do not include personal information in a note unless you want us to have it.

Notes are sent over an encrypted connection, delivered through Google Forms, and stored in a Google spreadsheet that only we can access. The spreadsheet also records the date and time each note arrives. As with any internet request, Google receives your device's IP address and the names of the game engine and operating system that sent the request; we do not receive these, and Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. We use notes only to fix problems and improve the game. We do not sell or share them.

Beta feedback form

Our website has an optional beta feedback form. If you fill it in and send it, we receive what you enter: the type of feedback, your message, and any optional answers you give (an enjoyment rating, how far you have got, time played, platform, game version, device, and an email address if you want a reply). Your browser also sends how long the page was open before you sent it and a random submission number, which we use only to block spam and duplicate sends.

Submissions are sent over an encrypted connection, delivered through Google Apps Script, and stored in a Google spreadsheet that only we can access, with the date and time each one arrives. Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of the request; we do not receive these. If you leave the email box empty, the submission is not linked to your name or any account. If you give an email address, we use it only to follow up on that submission. We use submissions only to fix problems and improve the game. We do not sell or share them.

Emails to support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and we use them only to answer you. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

Purchases

Idle Microbes is sold through Google Play and Steam. Google or Valve processes your purchase; we do not receive your payment details. Google Play and the Steam client record information about your purchases and your use of their services under their own policies: see the Google Privacy Policy and the Steam Privacy Policy.

Children

Idle Microbes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a feedback note or through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

We never receive your saved game. Uninstalling Idle Microbes deletes it from an Android device; on a computer, delete the game's data folder. A copy in your Android device backup is managed in your Google account.

To delete a feedback note or feedback form submission you sent:

  1. Email support@formalizedchaos.com with the subject "Idle Microbes data deletion".
  2. Include the approximate date you sent it and some of its wording (or the email address you gave in the form), so we can find it. Notes carry no name or account, so this is the only way to identify yours.

We delete the note and its context line from our spreadsheet within 30 days and reply to confirm. We keep no copy. Apart from emails you send us, feedback notes and feedback form submissions are the only data about you that we hold.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
